Home / Capabilities

MANUFACTURING CAPABILITIES

Advanced precision machining, multi-axis CNC manufacturing, and specialized engineering solutions built for accuracy, consistency, and reliable performance.

CNC Turning & Swiss Screw

High-precision CNC turning for shafts, pins, bushings, and complex components with tight dimensional control.

  • Capacity: Ø3 mm to Ø250 mm × 750 mm
  • Spindle Speed: Up to 6,000 RPM
  • Accuracy: Runout < 0.003 mm
  • Automation: Automatic bar feeding

4 & 5-Axis VMC Milling

Multi-axis CNC milling for complex components, hydraulic parts, manifolds, brackets, and precision profiles

  • Working Envelope: 850 × 510 × 510 mm
  • Spindle: Up to 15,000 RPM
  • Inspection: In-process optical probing
  • Tolerances: Tight positional tolerances

Gear Hobbing & Broaching

Precision manufacturing of spur gears, helical gears, pinions, internal splines, and other power-transmission components.

  • Module Range: 1.0 to 12.0
  • Gear Diameter: Up to Ø800 mm
  • Gear Standards: DIN / AGMA
  • Heat Treatment: Case hardening

Rollers & Shaft Systems

Custom-engineered rollers and shafts for material handling, packaging, steel mills, and industrial conveyor applications.

  • Diameter: Ø50 mm to Ø400 mm
  • Balancing: ISO 1940 Grade G2.5
  • Length: Up to 3.5 meters
  • Surface-Options: Hard chrome, black oxide, rubber lagging

Cylindrical & Surface Grinding

Precision grinding for bearing journals, guide shafts, and critical components requiring fine surface finishes.

  • Surface Finish: Ra 0.2 μm
  • Grinding: Cylindrical & surface
  • Parallelism: Within 0.002 mm
  • Roundness: TIR < 0.0015 mm

Thread Rolling & Whirling

Cold-forming solutions for lead screws, Acme threads, trapezoidal threads, and high-strength fasteners.

  • Thread Range: Trapezoidal & Acme
  • Rolling Force: Up to 30 tons
  • Straightness: 0.05 mm/m maximum
  • Applications: Lead screws, fasteners & threaded components
