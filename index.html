Home / Capabilities

MANUFACTURING CAPABILITIES

Multi-axis CNC turning, 5-axis synchronous VMC milling, gear hobbing, cylindrical grinding, and heavy fabrication.

CNC Turning & Swiss Screw

High-speed precision turning on twin-spindle CNC lathes with driven live tooling for simultaneous C-axis milling and cross-drilling in one setup.

  • Capacity: Ø3 mm to Ø250 mm x 750 mm length
  • Spindle Speed: Up to 6,000 RPM
  • Accuracy: Runout < 0.003 mm, Concentricity < 0.002 mm
  • Automation: 3-meter automatic magazine bar feeder

4 & 5-Axis VMC Milling

Simultaneous multi-axis prismatic machining of complex hydraulic fluid manifolds, pump housings, aerospace brackets, and intricate contours.

  • Working Envelope: 850 x 510 x 510 mm travel
  • Spindle: 15,000 RPM direct-drive with cooling
  • Inspection: Renishaw OMP60 optical tool probes
  • Tolerances: True position within 0.008 mm

Gear Hobbing & Broaching

Precision gear cutting, spline broaching, and CNC tooth flank grinding for spur gears, helical gears, pinions, and internal splines.

  • Module Range: Module 1.0 to 12.0 (Up to Ø800 mm)
  • Accuracy Standard: DIN 3962 Class 6 / AGMA 12
  • Heat Treatment: Case hardening (58-62 HRC)
  • Spline Standards: DIN 5480, DIN 5482, ANSI B92.1

Rollers & Shaft Systems

Custom engineered industrial rollers for packaging, steel mills, automated sorters, and high-speed material handling conveyor systems.

  • Dimensions: Ø50 mm to Ø400 mm (Length to 3.5m)
  • Balancing: ISO 1940 Grade G2.5 dynamic balancing
  • Surface Coating: Hard chrome, black oxide, rubber lagging
  • Bearing Journals: Ground for 6200/6300 series bearings

Cylindrical & Surface Grinding

Sub-micron finish grinding for bearing journals, guide shafts, and precision spacers requiring mirror surface roughness.

  • Surface Finish: Ra 0.2 µm mirror finish
  • Types: CNC Centerless, Cylindrical, Surface
  • Parallelism: Within 0.002 mm across 500 mm
  • Roundness: Total Indicator Reading < 0.0015 mm

Thread Rolling & Whirling

Cold-forming hydraulic thread rolling for lead screws, acme threads, and high-tensile fasteners, creating unbroken grain flow for fatigue resistance.

  • Thread Types: Trapezoidal Tr & Acme (Tr 10x2 to 100x12)
  • Force Capacity: 30-Ton hydraulic rolling force
  • Straightness: 0.05 mm runout per meter maximum
  • Mating Nuts: High-grade Phosphor Bronze RG7 / PB1
